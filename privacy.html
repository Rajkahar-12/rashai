RashAI Privacy

What we store: RashAI does not keep your chats on its server. Your chat history is saved only in your own browser, and you can delete any chat with the ✕ button or by clearing your browser data.

What we send: To answer you, your messages are sent to a third-party AI service that generates the reply. Do not share passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other sensitive information in chats.

Tracking: RashAI has no ads, no accounts and no analytics trackers.

AI notice: RashAI is an AI, not a human, and it can make mistakes. Check important information.

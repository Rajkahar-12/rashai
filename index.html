About RashAI

RashAI is a premium, focused study companion designed to help students understand complex topics, prepare for exams, debug code, and create revision notes.

Our Mission

We believe learning should be frictionless. RashAI strips away the clutter and delivers accurate, tailored educational answers with beautiful formatting, mathematical equations, and downloadable study materials.

Features

  • Interactive AI chat for concepts and exam prep.
  • Beautiful LaTeX rendering for math and science.
  • One-click PDF and TXT exports of answers.
  • Project file extraction (ZIP downloads for code).

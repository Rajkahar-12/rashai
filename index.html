About RashAI

RashAI is a premium, focused study companion designed to help students understand complex topics, prepare for exams, debug code, and create revision notes.

Our Mission

We believe learning should be frictionless. RashAI strips away the clutter and delivers accurate, tailored educational answers with beautiful formatting, mathematical equations, and downloadable study materials.

Features

  • Interactive AI chat for concepts and exam prep.
  • Beautiful LaTeX rendering for math and science.
  • One-click PDF and TXT exports of answers.
  • Project file extraction (ZIP downloads for code).
  • Multi-model answers combined into one final response.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

RashAI can be used as a guest without an account. If you choose to sign in, we collect your name, email address, or phone number through Firebase Authentication to create and secure your account. For signed-in users, chat history is stored in Firebase Firestore under your user ID. Guest chat history is stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your messages and attached files are sent to our secure backend (and the underlying AI providers) solely to generate a response. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Retention

Guest chats are saved on your device, not on our servers. Signed-in users' chats are stored in Firebase Firestore and can be deleted at any time using the "Clear chat history" button in the sidebar.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics scripts. Firebase Authentication may store session information on your device. Your theme preferences and local chat history are stored on your device.
